# Brand bible | The Green Pen Man

## The promise
**Little drawings. Lessons worth remembering.**

Make people *a little wiser* in a few seconds by pairing memorable practical wisdom with an illustration that reveals why the advice is true. Warm in presentation, sharp in thought.

## Identity
- Name: **The Green Pen Man** (approved). The brand persona is a thoughtful, slightly enigmatic observer with a green pen, not necessarily a literal on-post mascot.
- Core product: evergreen illustrated life advice, told through one-sentence proverbs and compact parables.
- Reader feeling: "That's painfully true," "I needed to hear that," "I know who should see this," or "I will remember that."
- Avoid: motivational poster, cynicism machine, therapy-speak, fortune-cookie vagueness, animal-art fan page, moral superiority, faux-academic philosophy.
- Brand voice: quietly authoritative, plainspoken yet poetic, warm not cloying, witty when apt, capable of severity without aggression.
- Publisher posture: let the drawing and line lead; do not explain a joke within the image.

## Reader benefit
Each post is a short decision tool: understand incentives, set boundaries, resist manipulation, accept trade-offs, perceive truth, protect independence, recognize habits, choose people, and live with courage and generosity.

## Meaning before art
Do not select a crow because crows are pretty. Select a crow only if it makes the point more immediate than a person or an object would. The sentence must be intelligible independently; the image gives it emotional or intellectual force.

## Brand consistency
- Primary green family, no pink borrowed from inspiration creators.
- Elegant and quiet, not precious or excessively detailed.
- Distinctive single-scene linework. No watermark or logo on the artwork until owner approves a specific treatment.
- Human/animal/object flexibility is a feature, not a deviation.
- Publication name can appear in profile/metadata without being stamped prominently on every post.

## Caption policy
The caption is the direct "why it matters" text. Write it with actual substance and implications. Usually 1–2 sentences; no "Why it matters:" label required. Do not restate the metaphor without any additional insight.

## Future extensions (ideas, not approved products)
Illustrated proverb book, art prints, notebooks, paper goods, recurring journal/email edition. Do not build, launch, or market these without a separate decision.

## Open decisions
Exact Instagram handle and bio, final font identification/license, definitive hex values sampled from the approved original raster images, publication cadence, soundtrack strategy, avatar, commercial products, production automation. Never invent decisions to fill gaps.
