# Prompt master | Single-post art direction

## Precondition
Have an approved or clearly designated copy draft, short plain-language lesson, and an art brief specifying what must be visibly true in the composition. Read `visual/VISUAL_STANDARD.md` and inspect approved images. Do not ask the generator for "8 sample posters" when the user wants individual posts.

## Image-only prompt, then precise typography (preferred)
```text
Create EXACTLY ONE finished portrait illustration for The Green Pen Man,
NOT a grid, collage, contact sheet, gallery, storyboard, or multi-panel layout.
Canvas: 4:5 upright, intended final export 1080x1350.

Scene: [ONE SPECIFIC VISUAL EVENT; 1-3 focal subjects; their posture and relationship].
Core truth communicated visually: [TRUTH IN PLAIN WORDS].
Required read at thumbnail size: [VISUAL CONTRADICTION OR CRITICAL DETAIL].
Do not depict: [MISINTERPRETATIONS].

House art direction: warm soft pale sage textured paper background (#DAE2B8 approximate),
delicate deep forest-green (#30492B approximate) hand-inked linework with
slightly imperfect contour and restrained hatch detail, optional muted
olive-green wash (#8B9D72 approximate). ONLY 2-3 GREEN SHADES TOTAL.
Authentic intimate indie-comic / restrained pencil-engraving spirit,
not ornate, photo-realistic or artificially sentimental.

Composition: generous negative space, one concentrated illustration
in the middle/lower half; upper 25-30% left visually clear for text.
NO words, captions, letters, labels, logos, borders, other panels,
background vignette clutter, unrelated landscape, pink, brown or blue.
Make the visual intellectually precise, original and immediately legible.
```

## Typeset exact approved sentence separately
- Insert the **exact on-image sentence**, not a paraphrase. Check capitalization and punctuation.
- Dark forest-green elegant italic serif, lightly cursive in character, centered; legible on a phone; no ornate flourishes.
- Save editable layered file or source and flattened PNG. Do not distribute fonts.
- Do not place the "why it matters" explanation inside the image.

## One-pass fallback when a compositor is unavailable
```text
Produce exactly ONE vertical 4:5 poster (NOT a grid or collage) in
the locked pale sage / deep forest green fine-engraved style.
ONE central minimalist metaphorical scene: [SCENE].
At top, elegantly typeset EXACTLY THIS ONE SENTENCE in legible
italic literary serif, without altering any word:
"[EXACT APPROVED SENTENCE]"
Ensure no other text appears anywhere. Preserve large quiet margins.
```
**Always transcribe the actual render and proofread before approval.** If it changes copy or returns a grid, it is an unapproved draft, not a publication asset.

## Negative prompt ideas
No multiple posters; no grids; no collage; no watermark; no faux marble;
no photorealism; no scenic mountains unless semantically necessary; no excess vines;
no cartoon tear drops; no smiling mascots; no gradients/glows; no extra colors;
no elaborate medieval border; no giant headings; no decorative type.
