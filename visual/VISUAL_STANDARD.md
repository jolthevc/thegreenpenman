# Visual standard | Pale-green illustrated wisdom

## The house look (locked direction)

**Single portrait artwork.** Warm pale sage/pistachio paper; rich dark forest/olive green type and contours; optional middle green for form. Restrained hand-made texture. Detailed enough to feel human, but never busy. Simplicity and *semantic sharpness* are the goals.

### Palette
Use ONLY **two or three related green shades** in the post, unless founder explicitly changes the standard. Starting approximations, **not** colorimetrically verified brand swatches:
- Paper: `#DAE2B8` (soft light sage, warm olive undertone)
- Primary ink and typography: `#30492B` (deep leafy forest)
- Optional wash/shade: `#8B9D72` (muted botanical olive)
Sample exact final green values from approved original image once imported; then freeze them in a versioned update. No rosy/pink, warm brown, golden sunsets, blue skies, multicolor props or black text by default.

### Drawing technique
Fine slightly irregular pen/etching or personal-sketchbook linework. Subtle imperfect contours and crosshatching; quiet natural anatomy and ordinary gestures. Authentic-looking paper fiber, **very restrained**. Do not over-texturize or imitate identifiable artists. Aim for intimate, accessible, gently old-fashioned, not lavishly antique.

### Composition
- 4:5 **1080x1350px** feed export by default (confirm platform placement if different).
- One central readable metaphor, dominant enough to recognize on a phone.
- The sentence has a stable quiet top zone; illustration generally below. Let relative sizing flex with phrase length without obscuring the art.
- Approximate safe zone: keep all text / critical details >8% from left/right and >7% from top/bottom. Preview as small phone thumbnails and cropped feed.
- Vast clean negative space is deliberate; fill nothing merely for prettiness.
- Background/environment: only if necessary to clarify the message. No unnecessary cypress hills, architecture, flourish borders, cart wheels, vines, decorative stars or faux scenic painting.
- Absolutely no contact sheets, multi-post grids or panel collage unless a **comparison contact sheet** is explicitly commissioned separately; those are never final posts.

### Typography
Dark green, elegant **readable** italic serif or gentle calligraphic serif; understated cursive *flavor*, not hard-to-read joined cursive, swooping swashes, generic marker handwriting or ornate wedding calligraphy. Generally centered, balanced line breaks with 2–4 lines as needed. Phrase itself is **one grammatical sentence**, however many visual lines it wraps into. Keep type uniform across posts.
Set exact approved type **verbatim**. Prefer adding text by a reliable compositor after generating image-only art rather than trusting AI to spell it. Save editable source when possible. Never distribute font files.

### Image selection
Use humans, animals or objects, whichever serves the insight. Tell an entire *relationship* through simple staging: the umbrella salesman benefits from predicted storm, the lion is content while others hide, the bird faces an open cage door. Avoid stock symbols and forced animal cuteness.

### Visual no-go examples
Multicolored fairy tales, large fake teary eyes, busy backdrops, glossy or airbrushed AI skin/fur, cinematic 3D, Victorian ornament frames, realistic photography, excessive crosshatching filling all whitespace, text too large, text errors, text competing with art, repeated template scenes without concept differentiation.

### Reference authority
**Actual original** files in `references/approved/` (after they're imported) outrank all text adjectives in this doc on nuanced ink texture and layout; do NOT copy individual subjects or expressions from reference. The work must be new even if the technique is consistent.

### Output checklist
One image, correct aspect ratio, exact phrase, 2–3 green shades, readable elegant typography, compelling clear metaphor, no unnecessary embellishment, thumbnail legibility, spelling/punctuation checked, no stray symbols. Keep a source/master copy and web export.
