# Editorial territories and range

Every post needs a useful life lesson. These are **subjects**, not formulaic hashtags or hard quotas.

1. **Human nature and incentives:** self-interest, persuasion, conflicts of interest, misplaced trust, reciprocity, temptation. E.g., umbrella seller under blue sky.
2. **Relationships and boundaries:** reciprocity, trust, sacrifice, emotional labor, codependency, repair. E.g., a woman setting down luggage belonging to others.
3. **Power and authority:** silence vs consent, punished truth, insecure status, governance, intimidation. E.g., lion amid animals who hide.
4. **Freedom, risk and comfort:** dependence, familiar cages, protective fences, opportunity cost, safety vs agency. E.g., open cage or moored boat.
5. **Self-deception and identity:** appearances, rationalization, ego, masks, vanity, comparison.
6. **Time, regret and priorities:** procrastination, irreversible choices, patience, opportunity cost, seasons of life.
7. **Courage, kindness, integrity and friendship:** earned hope, service, goodness without naivety, standing firm. Avoid being all dark.

### Editorial balance
No hard percentages. Rotate emotional valence: warning, illumination, humor, tenderness, courage, melancholy, relief. Look at the most recent 10 posts before selecting the next: do not publish five cages/wolves in a row. Avoid forcing a human/animal/object ratio; choose the clearest metaphor.

### Idea discovery questions
- Who benefits when this situation persists?
- What seems protective but has a hidden cost?
- What do people mistake for love, peace, respect or progress?
- What kind of kindness is harder than it looks?
- What trap works because the victim wants what it offers?
- Which admirable quality becomes destructive past a certain point?
- What could a person in this situation fail to see?
- What would you tell a younger friend in one unforgettable sentence?

### Reject ideas if
They are not actionable, rely on a weak or already-overused metaphor, need too much explanation to decode, promote cruelty/paranoia as wisdom, make exaggerated claims, or feel like generic motivational/social-therapy content.
