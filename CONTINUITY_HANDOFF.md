## Historical lessons learned
- Original inspiration was an artist's intimate animal illustrations with striking lines; we explicitly moved away from copying their pink background, particular wolf/snake composition, expressive conventions or text treatments.
- Elaborate medieval manuscripts, painterly fantasy scenes, intricate backgrounds, fake cinematic lighting, grids/contact sheets, enormous sad cartoon eyes, generic motivational quotes and two-sentence pseudo-parables were rejected.
- Only 2-3 green shades and consistent elegant italic typography were requested after experimenting with many styles.
- Generators have repeatedly returned multi-panel contact sheets despite requests for standalone images; do not treat a grid as a finished Instagram post.
- "Why it matters" should be **the caption text itself**, not a heading placed in the post image.
- Some observations were too passive; founder explicitly selected **directive advice** as the lead style.
