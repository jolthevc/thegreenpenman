# Production workflow | Idea to publication

**Editorial approval is a gate, not an afterthought.**

## Phase 0: Read and orient
Read `AGENTS.md` and `CONTINUITY_HANDOFF.md`. Inspect approved reference images. Check registry for duplicates and approvals. Name uncertainties openly.

## Phase 1: Insight
Start from a defensible life lesson. Record it in plain words before poetic phrasing. Determine whether it's worth readers remembering.

## Phase 2: Copy
Write 3–5 **one-sentence** directive-parable approaches. Cut clichés. Select strongest. Pair with a 1–2 sentence direct caption. Score by `QUALITY_GATE.md`. Status: `copy_draft`. Obtain explicit copy approval or delegated editorial authority before production. Save exact words.

## Phase 3: Visual metaphor
Brainstorm a **human**, **animal**, and **object** way to illustrate when the concept permits. Select the strongest, not the most photogenic. Describe the exact physical relationship that delivers the lesson. Reject scenes that reverse or muddy the message.

## Phase 4: Render
Use `visual/IMAGE_PROMPT_MASTER.md`. One standalone 4:5 poster per requested post. Prefer image-only art + deterministic text composition for exact copy. Do not batch unrelated messages into one generator prompt, and no contact-sheet outputs as deliverables. Status: `art_draft`.

## Phase 5: QA and revision
Check literal copy, grammar, visual metaphor, type legibility, green-only palette, blank space, human authenticity, crop safety, artwork originality and practical caption meaning. Zoom to 25% and preview 4:5 feed crop. Use 0–5 scoring and hard gates. If weak, revise precisely what is wrong. Never fake approvals.

## Phase 6: Approval and package
After owner selects exact image: mark art-approved and archive its source/master. Package:
- final `1080x1350.png`
- approved one-sentence on-image text
- exact caption `caption.txt`
- source or prompt and references
- metadata `post.json` (id, status, subject, copy, quality notes, provenance)
- optional alternate formats, only if requested.
Status: `package_ready`.

## Phase 7: Publish
Only on instruction / with configured authorized publication. Record date, platform ID/URL, published caption, and any deviations. Status: `published`. Never claim published from export completion.

## Phase 8: Learning
Track reach, saves, sends/shares, profile visits, follow conversion, and qualitative comments if available. Look for *types of insights* and *visual clarity*, not just engagement volume. Don't chase cynical hooks at cost of truth.

## Handoff
Update registry, new reference manifest entries (when exact art approved), and `CONTINUITY_HANDOFF.md` with decisions, asset paths, and next steps.
