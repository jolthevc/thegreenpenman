# The Green Pen Man

**Little drawings. Lessons worth remembering.**

An editorial-first illustrated wisdom publication. One sentence of useful, memorable life advice. One deceptively simple sage-green illustration. One direct caption that explains why it matters.

> **New agent? Start at [AGENTS.md](AGENTS.md). Read every required document before proposing, illustrating, or publishing anything.**

## The format

1. **Insight:** An actual lesson about how people, relationships, incentives, power, decisions, or life work.
2. **On-image line:** Exactly **one grammatical sentence**, typically prescriptive (warning, command, counsel, or rule of thumb), poetic but comprehensible at first reading.
3. **Visual:** One concept, one clean illustration; animals, people, and objects are all eligible. Choose the best subject for the lesson, not a quota.
4. **Caption:** One or two plain, precise sentences explaining the practical insight, without merely repeating the line.
5. **Approval:** Nothing is considered final merely because an AI drafted or generated it.

## Current decisions

- **Brand name:** The Green Pen Man, locked October 17, 2026.
- **Style:** Pale warm sage-green textured ground, darker botanical/forest-green fine ink engraving and tonal linework, a third green only if needed; elegant legible italic serif lettering; generous negative space; clean single-scene compositions.
- **Voice:** One-sentence **directive parables** that tell readers how to live, what to avoid, or what to notice. Unexpected, substantial, kind without being saccharine, sometimes cutting.
- **Subject:** People, animals, objects, or landscapes as appropriate. Never force all-animal fables.
- **Captions:** Directly state the "why it matters" in plain language.
- **Primary platform:** Instagram, portrait-first; default draft canvas 1080 x 1350 (4:5). Reels adaptation is optional, not yet a locked requirement.
- **Approved visual precedent:** Green bird/open-cage, woman setting down luggage, umbrella seller, lion in quiet forest, sheep at fence, boat at harbor. These are *style direction examples*, not an instruction to reuse their scenes.
- **Important limitation:** Original raster files are staged for GitHub upload separately. Do not claim files are on GitHub until visible under `references/approved/`. See [reference instructions](references/README.md).
